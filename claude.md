toward that. Design serves function — but it should still look 
premium enough that a homeowner trusts this company with their garden.

## Design
- Extract primary and accent colors from assets/logo.png
- Use Playfair Display for headings, DM Sans for body
- Dark premium feel — deep backgrounds, not flat white
- Clean generous whitespace — premium not cluttered
- Consistent border radius and spacing throughout
- Mobile first — fully responsive at 320px, 768px, 1024px+

## Animations — Purposeful Only
- Hero content staggered fade-in on load (headline, sub, CTAs)
- Sections fade up on scroll into view
- Service cards subtle lift on hover
- Gallery images slight zoom on hover
- Nothing else — no gimmicks

## Conversion Rules — NON NEGOTIABLE
- Phone number visible at all times in sticky nav
- Phone number clickable tel: link everywhere it appears
- Minimum 3 CTAs on the page
- Contact form: name, service, message only — nothing else
- Trust signals visible above the fold
- Real photos only

## Do Not
- No em dashes in any copy
- No generic AI copy — write like a real Manchester tradesman's website
- No heavy libraries or frameworks
- No animations that slow the page down
- No stock photos
